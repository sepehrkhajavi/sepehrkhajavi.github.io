About

Sepehr Khajavi

I’m an architectural designer and 3D visualization artist with a strong passion for architecture, spatial design, and visual storytelling.

As a newcomer to the profession, I approach each project with curiosity, energy, and a constant desire to learn. My experience includes architectural modeling, rendering, interior and exterior design studies, and professional collaborations on residential and commercial projects.

I see the early stage of my career as an opportunity to explore, improve, and develop a clear design identity. I’m especially interested in the relationship between form, material, light, and atmosphere, and I enjoy transforming ideas into detailed and engaging visual experiences. With every project, my goal is to strengthen both my technical skills and architectural thinking while creating work that is thoughtful, contemporary, and visually compelling.
